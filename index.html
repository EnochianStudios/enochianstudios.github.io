I. Field Deployment (Operations Manual)

ParaNode is a passive environmental monitoring tool. For accurate forensic results, adhere to the following deployment protocol:

  1. Isolate: Place your device on a flat, stable surface away from power outlets, breaker boxes, or active electronics to prevent false positives.
  2. Calibrate: Press 'Calibrate' to establish a dynamic baseline. This squelches the natural "noise" of the room's baseline electromagnetic field.
  3. Monitor: Observe the real-time graph for anomalous spikes breaking the established threshold.

II. Environmental Diagnostics (EMF)

Utilizing the high-fidelity magnetometer found in modern flagship devices, ParaNode provides real-time, triple-axis visualization of electromagnetic flux.

  • Microtesla (µT) Precision: Track fluctuations with sub-millisecond refresh rates.
  • Adjustable Sensitivity: Fine-tune the sensor gate to filter out micro-fluctuations or capture the slightest anomalies.
  • Hardware Requirement: A physical Magnetometer (Compass) sensor is required. If your device lacks this hardware, EMF functionality is disabled.

III. Diagnostic Modes

Toggle between primary analysis engines depending on your investigation parameters:

IV. Forensic Data Analysis (Pro)
